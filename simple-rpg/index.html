Low half resolution, no effects · Medium shadows, sword trails, hit particles · High grass, flowers, glow · Ultra animated grass that reacts to you, weather, screen shake, and a GPU (WebGL) post-processing pass: bloom, color grading, vignette.
Render scale: draws the game at a lower resolution and enlarges it on the GPU with a bicubic filter plus contrast-adaptive sharpening (an FSR-style upscaler). Lower scales run faster on weak GPUs or 4K screens; 75% is a good start. It needs WebGL and is not used on Low quality.
Frame generation: the game simulates fewer times per second and fills the gaps with generated (interpolated) frames — 3× means 1 simulated frame and 2 generated ones. Saves CPU, but each step adds input delay, so 2× or 3× is the sweet spot.
Reduce flashing & shake: removes white screen flashes, blinking, screen shake and hit freeze-frames.
