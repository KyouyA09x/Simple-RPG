# Update log

## 0.3.1
- **A new opening:** the story begins in a village that does not survive the night.
- **Prestige** is the new name for rebirth, and every prestige raises the ceiling on crit chance and crit damage.
- **Crits are rare:** lower base crit, lower crit rolls on gear, and the Cooldown and Critical stats are gone (their points are given back).
- **Tougher monsters:** more health on every difficulty.
- **Creature moves:** mutated and evolved creatures use moves of their own, shown before they land. Some creatures resist or ignore burning, poison, bleeding, cold or stun.
- **Potions:** Swiftness and Ward at the Alchemist.
- **Quests:** a Quests window on the Tab wheel, and new escort and delivery quests.
- **Inns:** rent a room (every door has a sign) and sleep in your own bed to wake well rested for 10 minutes. The innkeepers and the guild share rumours, some of which start quests or foretell a stone door.
- **Villagers:** the Saintess joins the church, and the villagers look more varied, with hair that moves.
- **Installer:** a Windows installer, as well as the zip.

## 0.3.0
- **Dungeons:** a building (ruined cathedral, cave mouth, mausoleum, broken tower, well or stone gate) opens now and then after a wave. Three to five floors of sleeping elites and evolved creatures in mazes, rooms, ring halls and crypts, with sunlight or moonlight through the ceiling. The evolved boss waits alone on its own floor, and dungeon loot is always Polished or better.
- **Ranged weapons:** bows and staves with their own attacks.
- **Boss weapons:** each of the eight bosses can drop its own signature weapon.
- **Looks:** katanas and whips come in many colours, and katana guards in several shapes.
- **Creatures:** idle fidgets and a unique death scene for every creature.
- **Drops:** runes and essences are rarer.
- **Main menu:** the version number and an update log.
- **Dev menu:** tools for dungeons, boss weapons and creatures.

## 0.2.0
- **Runes and essences:** five rune tiers, the Enchantress, and weapon effects found as essences from elites and bosses.
- **Biome creatures:** every creature lives in its own biome, with evolutions and pack leaders.
- **Village:** a bigger village, a church with a priestess for rebirth, and a quest board.
- **Return Scroll** and a utility wheel (hold R).
- **Ailments** and a healing potion that cures them; new inventory tabs, settings tabs, music and sound overhaul.

## 0.1.0
- First public release: waves, eight biomes and bosses, villages, gear, rebirth, and a downloadable Windows build.
